MicType for iPhone — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

MicType is a local-first voice keyboard. Core principle: your voice and text belong to you.

  1. We collect no personal data, run no servers of our own, and do not track you.
  2. Voice & transcription. Recording and speech-to-text run entirely on your device. Audio never leaves your device and is never uploaded.
  3. AI features (optional). When — and only when — you actively use polish / translate / rewrite / voice command, the app sends the specific text you dictated or selected (never your audio), using your own API key, directly from your device to the third-party, OpenAI-compatible AI endpoint you configured (for example OpenAI, another provider, or your own self-hosted server). This text is used solely to return the processed result to you. MicType has no servers of its own and neither relays nor stores this text. Because you choose and control which endpoint is used, that third party's collection and use of the text is governed by its own privacy policy and security terms; we recommend reviewing the privacy and security terms of the provider you configure before enabling AI.
  4. Microphone. The microphone is captured only when you tap the record button. iOS shows the orange microphone indicator while a session is active; capture stops when the app is closed.
  5. Keyboard Full Access. Used to read the local transcription result from the app (via App Group) and to call the AI endpoint you configured. The keyboard does not record audio and does not upload keystrokes.
  6. Local storage. Your API key is stored in the device Keychain; your input history and vocabulary stay on your device.
  7. Children. MicType is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.

MicType iPhone 版 — 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 6 月 24 日

MicType 是一款本地优先的语音输入键盘。核心原则：你的语音与文本属于你。

  1. 我们不收集你的个人数据，没有自有服务器，不做任何用户追踪。
  2. 语音与转写。录音与语音转文字全程在你的设备本地完成，音频不会离开设备、不上传任何服务器。
  3. AI 功能（可选）。仅当你主动使用润色 / 翻译 / 改写 / 语音指令时，App 才会把你听写或选中的文本（绝不包含音频），经你自己配置的 API Key，从你的设备直接发送到你所配置的第三方 OpenAI 兼容 AI 端点（例如 OpenAI、其它服务商，或你自建的服务器）。这些文本仅用于把处理结果返回给你。MicType 没有自有服务器，不经手、不存储这些文本。由于端点由你选择与掌控，该第三方如何收集与使用这些文本，由其自身的隐私政策与安全条款约定；建议你在启用 AI 前先查看所配置服务商的隐私与安全条款。
  4. 麦克风。麦克风仅在你点击录音键时捕获声音；会话期间 iOS 会显示橙色指示；关闭 App 即停止。
  5. 键盘完全访问。用于读取 App 内的本地转写结果（通过 App Group），以及调用你配置的 AI 端点。键盘不录音、不上传你的按键。
  6. 本地存储。你的 API Key 保存在设备钥匙串（Keychain）；输入历史与词汇表仅保存在你的设备本地。
  7. 儿童。MicType 不面向儿童，也不向任何人收集数据。

变更

本政策如有变更，我们将更新上方的生效日期；重大变更会在 App 的更新说明中体现。
