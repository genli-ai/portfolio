MicType for iPhone — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

MicType is a voice keyboard that runs on your own AI. Core principle: your voice and text belong to you, and they never pass through us.

  1. We collect no personal data, run no servers of our own, and do not track you. MicType has no accounts and no sign-in.
  2. Voice & transcription. What happens to a recording depends on whether you have connected an AI provider:
    • No provider connected (or the cloud is unavailable — no network, a provider error, or your AI trial has ended): recognition runs entirely on your iPhone and the recording never leaves the device.
    • A provider connected, with your consent (see item 4): while you speak, the recording is streamed for recognition to the provider you chose — OpenAI, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, or DeepSeek — directly from your device, using your own API key. MicType has no servers and neither relays nor stores the audio. If that connection fails mid-sentence, recognition falls back to your iPhone so your words are not lost.
    • In app versions before 1.1.0, cloud recognition was a separate switch that was off by default; from 1.1.0 it is part of connecting a provider, which you do yourself and can undo at any time.
  3. AI features (text). When you dictate, or select or copy text and give a voice command, the app sends that text — and the terms you added to your own glossary, so names are spelled correctly — to the same provider, using the same key, directly from your device, solely to return the processed result to you. MicType neither relays nor stores this text.
  4. Your consent, and how to withdraw it. Before anything is sent, the app shows one consent dialog that names exactly what will leave the device (your recording while you speak, and the text you dictate, select or copy) and which provider it goes to. Nothing is sent until you accept. You can stop it at any time in Settings → Cloud AI → "Turn off cloud AI (remove key)", after which recognition runs on your iPhone again.
  5. The provider is your choice, and its terms apply. Because you choose the provider and supply your own key, that third party's collection and use of your audio and text is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. We recommend reading them before connecting. MicType is not a party to that relationship and receives nothing from it.
  6. Microphone. The microphone is captured only when you tap the record button; iOS shows the orange microphone indicator while a session is active; capture stops when the app is closed.
  7. Keyboard Full Access. Used to read the transcription result from the app (via the App Group) and to call the provider you configured. The keyboard does not record audio and does not upload keystrokes.
  8. Local storage. Your API key is stored in the device Keychain; your input history and glossary stay on your device and are never synced or uploaded.
  9. Children. MicType is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.
