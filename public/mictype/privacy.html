MicType for iPhone — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

MicType is a voice keyboard that runs on your own AI. Core principle: your voice and text belong to you, and they never pass through us.

  1. We collect no personal data, run no servers of our own, and do not track you. MicType has no accounts and no sign-in.
  2. Your provider, your key. To use MicType you connect an AI provider — OpenAI or Alibaba Cloud Model Studio — with an API key from your own account. Everything below goes directly from your device to that provider. We are not in the middle of it: MicType has no servers, and neither relays nor stores any of it.
  3. Voice. While you speak, your recording is streamed to that provider so your words can be recognised as you say them. If the connection fails mid-sentence, recognition falls back to your iPhone so your words are not lost.
  4. Text. The text you dictate, select or copy is sent to the same provider, with the same key, so it can be polished or so a voice command can act on it. The terms you added to your own glossary go along with it, so names are spelled correctly.
  5. Your consent, and how to withdraw it. Before anything is sent, the app shows one consent dialog that names exactly what will leave the device — your recording while you speak, and the text you dictate, select or copy — and which provider it goes to. Nothing is sent until you accept. To withdraw, clear the API key in Settings → AI provider; with no key, nothing leaves your device and dictation is recognised on your iPhone instead.
  6. The provider's own terms apply. Because you choose the provider and supply your own key, that third party's collection and use of your audio and text is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. We recommend reading them before connecting. MicType is not a party to that relationship and receives nothing from it.
  7. Microphone. The microphone is captured only when you tap the record button; iOS shows the orange microphone indicator while a session is active; capture stops when the app is closed.
  8. Keyboard Full Access. Used to read the transcription result from the app (via the App Group) and to call the provider you configured. The keyboard does not record audio and does not upload keystrokes.
  9. Local storage. Your API key is stored in the device Keychain; your input history and glossary stay on your device and are never synced or uploaded.
  10. Children. MicType is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.
